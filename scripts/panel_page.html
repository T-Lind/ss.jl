Suborbital profile

Orbit target

the goal is the orbit itself: ascent to the parking orbit, transfer burns on the kick stage where the target needs them, and the achieved elements reported against the request. GEO asks ~4.3 km/s of the kick stage — size it in the builder or expect an honest "propellant depleted".

Mission targets

nonzero TLI errors fly a dispersed cruise with an automatic mid-course correction at T+24 h

Lander & descent

—
the lander flies insertion, descent-orbit initiation and the descent on its own propellant; the launcher only has to throw its wet mass through TLI. Arrival is on a free return, so a failed insertion still comes home. By default it lands on the real thing: procedural terrain, mascons, orbit-determination error corrected by landing radar, and hazard avoidance choosing the touchdown point at high gate. Tick the box to take all of that away and watch what each of them was for.
