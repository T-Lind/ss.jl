Spacecraft viewer

Inspect the capsule or lunar lander before flying. Compare the exterior with the crew's view, check the windows and hatch, and explore each crew station. No mission run is needed.

Drag to orbit the exterior or look around the cockpit. Scroll or use + / − to zoom. Arrow keys rotate; R resets. Preview dimensions are independent of your vehicle settings. Cockpit controls are operated in the launch view.
